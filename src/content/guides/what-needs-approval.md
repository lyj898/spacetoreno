The same job can need a permit in one home and nothing at all in another. This chart covers five common works; the three guides after it go into each type of home.

## What the chart is based on

| Work | HDB flat | Condo | Landed house |
|---|---|---|---|
| Take down a non-structural wall | HDB permit: all hacking and demolition of walls needs HDB’s prior written approval | Not a BCA matter if the wall carries only its own weight; your by-laws may require MCST approval | No BCA plans (insignificant building work); URA needs no permission to enlarge rooms |
| Replace floor or wall tiles | HDB permit | No BCA plans; check your by-laws | No BCA plans, no URA permission |
| Replace windows | HDB permit, by a BCA approved window contractor | MCST approval for features on the outside of the building | No URA permission if floor area, setback and window position don’t change |
| Alter a structural wall, column or beam | Not allowed | Professional Engineer’s assessment and approval from the Commissioner of Building Control | Plans by a Qualified Person; structural plans by a civil or structural engineer |
| Enclose an open balcony | Not allowed | URA: balconies shall not be enclosed with walls or glass panels | URA planning permission needed |

The detail, with the exceptions:

- **[HDB renovation rules](/rules-and-permits/hdb-renovation-rules/)**: permits, HDB’s contractor directory, the 3-year bathroom rule for new flats and the works HDB never allows.
- **[Condo renovation rules](/rules-and-permits/condo-renovation-rules/)**: the MCST, by-laws, grilles, and when BCA or URA come in.
- **[Renovating a landed house](/rules-and-permits/landed-house-renovation/)**: URA’s exemptions, plan lodgment, Qualified Persons and party walls.

## Three rules that apply everywhere

1. **The owner answers for the work.** HDB says owners are responsible for their contractor following its guidelines; BCA says owners with unauthorised works have committed an offence; condo owners can be told by the MCST to undo unauthorised works at their own cost.
2. **Get approval before the work starts**, not after. A permit or approval granted afterwards is never guaranteed, and putting things back costs more than waiting.
3. **Some trades must be licensed, whatever the home.** Windows need an approved window contractor; wiring, gas and water pipes need licensed workers. See [licensed trades](/rules-and-permits/licensed-trades/).
