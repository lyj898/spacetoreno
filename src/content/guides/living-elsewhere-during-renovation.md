Whether to stay depends on how much is being done. Painting or new flooring in one room can be lived with. Hacking, a new kitchen or a new bathroom usually can’t: the dust gets everywhere, and for days or weeks you may have no working kitchen or bathroom.

## Signs you should move out

- Walls or floors are being hacked: HDB limits the noisiest work to weekdays, 9am to 5pm, but the dust lasts much longer.
- The kitchen or your only bathroom is being redone.
- The gas will be off: City Energy asks for the gas supply to be temporarily cut off and the meter removed during renovation.
- Someone at home is very young, elderly, unwell or works from home.

## Where to stay

- **Family or friends**, the most common answer.
- **A hotel**: URA says hotels have no minimum stay.
- **A serviced apartment**: URA sets a 7-night minimum.
- **A rented flat**: private homes and HDB flats can’t be let for stays shorter than 3 consecutive months, so this only works for a long renovation.

Ask your contractor for the schedule in writing before you book, and allow for slippage.

## Packing and storage

Anything left in the home during the works will get dusty, and may get damaged or in the way. Before the works start:

- **decide what stays, what goes into storage and what goes for good**, room by room;
- **clear out what you’re not keeping**, so it isn’t hacked around or packed up and stored for nothing;
- **move what you’re keeping** into storage, or into one room the contractor agrees to leave alone and seal off.

## Before you hand over the keys

- **Agree access**: who holds keys and access cards, and that work keeps to the permitted hours. See [working hours and neighbours](/during-the-works/renovation-working-hours/).
- **Check the permit and notices**: in HDB flats the Notice of Renovation must be displayed outside the flat until the works are done, and neighbours must have had written notice.
- **Photograph everything** you’re keeping, including built-ins and the common corridor outside your door.
- **Agree how you’ll check progress**: visits at each stage, before each [stage payment](/hiring/renovation-payment-schedule/).
- **Mark what mustn’t be touched**, such as the household shelter walls and door: see [the household shelter](/room-by-room/household-shelter/).

When the works are done, a proper clean comes before you move back in. OurKampung’s [after-renovation checklist](https://ourkampung.com/new-home/after-renovation-checklist/) covers what to check, clear and clean.
